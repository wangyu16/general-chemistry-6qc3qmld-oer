## 1. Prerequisites

You must have passed CHEM107 with a minimum grade of C.  

## 2. Course Description

General Chemistry II: Principles and problems of chemistry. This course continues the introduction to the properties of atoms and molecules and the reactions they undergo. Topics covered include properties of solutions, chemical kinetics, equilibrium, acid-base chemistry, precipitation, chemical thermodynamics, electrochemistry, and nuclear chemistry. This course is designed to give students the background necessary to answer both qualitative and quantitative questions relating to these topics.

## 3. Course Topics

- Intermolecular Forces and Liquids and Solids
- Physical Properties of Solutions
- Chemical Kinetics
- Chemical Equilibrium
- Acids and Bases
- Acid-Base Equilibria and Solubility Equilibria
- Thermodynamics
- Redox Reactions and Electrochemistry
- Nuclear Chemistry

## 5. Evaluation System

Your grade will be based on a weighted mean of the following components:

1. **Midterm Exams:** Three midterm exams, each worth 100 points. The lowest score will be dropped, making the total 200 points. Exam dates will be posted on the Moodle page. 
2. **Online assignments:** Worth a total of 150 points, calculated as follows: 
$$\text{Your points} = \frac{\text{Credits you earned from the assignments}}{\text{Maximum possible credits from the assignments}}\times 150$$
3. **Comprehensive final exam:** Worth 150 points. 

The final grade is calculated as
$$\text{Your Grade} = \frac{\text{Points you earned as described above}}{500} \times 100\% $$ 

You will be guaranteed at least the following grades for the percentages shown:  
* 90-100% - A  
* 80-89% - B  
* 70-79% - C  
* 60-69% - D  
* <60% - F
